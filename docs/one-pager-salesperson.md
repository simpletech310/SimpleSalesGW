# Salesperson — One-Page SOP

> **Role mission:** Hunt, qualify, close. Own every lead from first touch to signed MSA. *Aligned to portal v3.2.1.*

## You own
- Your leads from prospect → CLOSED_WON
- Logging every touch (calls, emails, meetings)
- Initiating the handoff packet to Ops

## You cannot
- See other reps' leads · Approve your own pricing below floor · Touch the customer side after handoff

---

## Daily rhythm

1. **Open `/`** — scan This-week's next-actions · Going-stale rail · Top opportunities
2. **Work the day** — every call/email/meeting logged on the lead's **Activity** tab with a Next-action + Due date
3. **End of day** — transcribe any paper Prospect intake cards into `/leads/new`
4. **Before signing off** — any lead idle 7+ days gets a decision: push, NURTURE, or CLOSED_LOST with reason

---

## Pipeline cheat sheet

| Stage | What it means | Move to next when… |
|---|---|---|
| LEAD | First touch logged | Real conversation + budget signal |
| QUALIFIED | DQ score solid, real opportunity | Discovery scheduled |
| DISCOVERY | Site survey running | Survey complete + scope clear |
| PRE_SALES | Building the quote | Approved price ready |
| PROPOSAL | Quote delivered | They acknowledge it |
| NEGOTIATION | Back-and-forth | Verbal yes / no |
| CLOSED_WON | Verbal yes captured | Initiate handoff immediately |
| CLOSED_LOST / NURTURE | Write the reason | n/a |

---

## Pricing — when to ask for approval

| Discount off MRR | Who approves | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5% | You | Note reason in the lead |
| **5–20%** | **Sales Manager** | Pricing card → **Request approval** |
| **20%+ or below floor** | **COO** | Same button — system auto-routes |

> Don't promise a price you can't yet approve. Send the request, wait for the green check, then commit to the customer.

## Handoff — the moment that matters

1. Set stage to **CLOSED_WON** with actual close date + final terms note
2. Click **Initiate handoff** — fill the structured form (start date, cutover, verbal commitments)
3. **Handoff QC** AI flags anything that conflicts with the MSP profile's out-of-scope list — clear those before submitting
4. Submit. COO accepts or rejects. **You see the decision on `/notifications`**
5. If rejected: read the reason, fix, resubmit. Don't argue — the COO's veto exists to protect your relationship with the customer

---

## Quick rules

- **Never edit an old activity.** Log a new one. The timeline must be honest.
- **No verbal promise the MSA doesn't cover.** If you must, list it on the handoff QC sheet so Ops doesn't get blindsided.
- **Photograph every site visit.** Rack rooms, IDF, doors, cabling pain. Upload to the lead's Documents tab same day.
- **Transcribe paper cards within 24 hours.** A card in your jacket pocket is a lost lead.
- **CLOSED_LOST needs a reason.** "Not interested" doesn't help anyone — write what actually killed it.
