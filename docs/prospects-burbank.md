# Gateway TelNet — Burbank-area prospect shortlist

**Compiled:** 2026-05-23
**Geography:** Greater Los Angeles (Burbank HQ + 25-mile radius — Glendale,
Pasadena, San Fernando Valley, Hollywood, Studio City, Sherman Oaks,
North Hollywood). Gateway TelNet office: 1212 S Victory Blvd, Burbank,
CA 91502.
**Target seat band:** 25–250 (MSP sweet spot; this is where co-managed IT,
voice/data convergence, and structured cabling pull-through revenue all
land at once).
**Why this list:** Each entry maps onto one of the 9 platform verticals
(`MEDICAL`, `LEGAL`, `FEDERAL_CONTRACTING`, `MANUFACTURING`, `HOSPITALITY`,
`FINANCIAL_SERVICES`, `PROFESSIONAL_SERVICES`, `EDUCATION`, `NONPROFIT`)
and at least one compliance overlay or known platform pain point that
Gateway sells against (HIPAA, CMMC/NIST 800-171, PCI, FERPA, voice/data
convergence, multi-site networking, MSP fatigue).

All entries are **public-record companies surfaced from web research** —
they are *not* pre-qualified opportunities. Lin should run each through
the standard Lead Qualification flow on the portal before any outreach.

## How to load into the portal

For each Tier-A row, Lin creates a Lead via **New Lead** with:
1. **Business name** + **industry** (one of the 9 enums)
2. **Address** (verify exact street + ZIP from public site)
3. **Compliance drivers** (multi-select: HIPAA, CMMC, PCI, etc.)
4. **Primary contact** — start with the role suggested in this doc; fill
   real name from LinkedIn / company site during research phase
5. **Research summary** — paste the "Why fit" + "Suggested bundle" lines
   from this doc as the starting note
6. Let the **auto-score** populate the Qualification Scorecard, then
   review/override the 8 dimensions on the QualificationCard

Once a lead is `QUALIFIED` (auto-score ≥ 30) the discovery flow opens up.
